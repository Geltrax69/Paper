# gun.mp4 — visual analysis and gameplay direction

Source: 1280 × 720, 60 fps, approximately 45 seconds. Examined samples across the clip plus a four-frame-per-second firing sequence.

## Observed

- First-person arena horde combat, with hand-drawn weapons, hands, enemies, crates and environmental props.
- Black-and-white crosshatching with saturated red hit splashes, a combo indicator, health, ammo and currency readouts.
- Rapid shooting and enemy pressure establish an energetic arcade rhythm.
- The largely white sky and almost black ground flatten depth. Dense ground texture competes with enemy silhouettes.
- In the sampled firing sequence, the large red marks dominate; muzzle flashes, projectile paths and individual impacts could communicate weapon behavior more clearly.

## Image direction

Preserve the paper-and-ink identity. Add warm ivory paper, violet distance and turquoise environmental accents, with golden flashes and red ink impacts. Keep strong enemy silhouettes and a clear center reticle.

Three concept boards, each containing two gameplay views:

1. Shotgun and pistols: broad pellet spread and heavy blast versus tight precise flashes and shell ejection.
2. Automatic gun and blaster: repeated tracer bursts versus luminous energy bolts and a circular impact pulse.
3. Throwables and throwing knives: an arcing grenade and an explosive ink burst versus a spinning blade with a short curved motion trail.

These are generated gameplay concept images. They are not captures of an implemented game, and gun.mp4 has not been modified.

## Suggested implementation behavior

- Shotgun: a wide irregular flash, a short recoil kick, pellet hit marks distributed across the target area, and a brief smoke curl.
- Pistol: a compact flash, one tracer and one impact with a quick recoil recovery.
- Automatic gun: intermittent visible tracers, short flashes and directional shell ejection, with controlled effect density.
- Blaster: a bright bolt core, colored halo, a short trail and a circular impact ripple.
- Grenade: a readable throw trajectory; concentrate the bright blast at the explosion center and push paper fragments outward.
- Throwing knife: a short glint and motion arc with a compact contact spark.

Effects should remain readable without hiding enemies or the reticle. If implemented, camera shake and flashes should have adjustable intensity.
