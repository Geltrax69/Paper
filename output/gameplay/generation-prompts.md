The exact prompts are recorded below.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: polished gameplay screenshot concept board for an arcade first-person horde shooter, inspired by the attached local video frame. Reference image is visual reference only. Preserve its hand-drawn black ink crosshatching, white paper weapon surfaces, comic-book outlines, first-person hands and weapons in foreground, open arena with crates and sketchy humanoid monster enemies, readable center aim and playful drawn HUD. Upgrade to vibrant saturated selective colors: golden yellow muzzle flash, scarlet ink hit marks, turquoise atmospheric accents and violet shadows, warm ivory paper sky. Enemies remain non-graphic stylized ink creatures, no anatomical gore. Crisp intentional game art, not photorealistic, not generic realistic military FPS. No title, branding or watermark. Include compact illustrated HUD with health 100, combo top left, small reticle and ammo bottom right. Small readable upper-right panel labels only. Overall board aspect 16:18. TOP PANEL label SHOTGUN: chunky white-paper double-barrel shotgun in lower right recoiling upward with a huge angular golden star-shaped muzzle blast, multiple distinct pellet streaks fanning toward three mid-distance ink monsters, energetic red/yellow impact starbursts, ejected shells and curling ink smoke. Bottom panel label PISTOLS: dual white-paper pistols held in left and right foreground, one firing with a tight yellow muzzle flash, a crisp white tracer to a target, ejected brass shell, small red impact mark, more precise and restrained effects than shotgun. Different arena angles and active enemies. Keep weapons large and beautifully rendered, make it feel playable and intensely vibrant.
