WASD move. Mouse aim. Click fire. R reload. Shift sprint. Space jump, press again midair for a double jump. Q dash. E hold grapple. F melee. Esc pause.

Desktop keyboard and mouse required. Touch controls are not available.

Wave 1 has 6 runners at speed 3.4. Each later wave adds 2 runners and 5% speed, up to 1.5x. A kill scores 100 points times the combo multiplier: x1, x2 at 3 combo, x3 at 5 combo. The combo lasts 4 seconds between kills. Wave bonus is 250 times the wave number. Death restarts the current wave with full health. Score and wave are kept, combo is lost. Health carries between waves, ammo refills. Health pickup restores 40, ammo pickup restores 3, both capped at their maximums. Sounds are synthesized with Web Audio. No audio files are used. The second jump resets only on landing. Grapple anchors sit on wall tops and crate tops within 14 units, need a clear line of sight, and detach when the rope is blocked. Melee deals 18 damage once per swing within 2.8 units, and only with a clear line of sight.

Settings

Shake scales damage camera shake. Flash scales the damage vignette and muzzle flash brightness. Reduced motion forces sprint scale 1 and no weapon bob. Volume and mute apply to all synthesized sounds. Audio starts only after Start is clicked.
