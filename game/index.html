WASD move. Mouse aim. Click fire. R reload. Shift sprint. Space jump, press again midair for a double jump. Q dash. E hold grapple. F melee. Esc pause.

Desktop keyboard and mouse required. Touch controls are not available.

How to play

Wave 1 has 6 runners at speed 3.4. Each later wave adds 2 runners and 5% speed, up to 1.5x. A kill scores 100 points times the combo multiplier: x1, x2 at 3 combo, x3 at 5 combo. The combo lasts 4 seconds between kills. Wave bonus is 250 times the wave number. Death restarts the current wave with full health. Score and wave are kept, combo is lost. Health carries between waves, ammo refills. Health pickup restores 40, ammo pickup restores 3, both capped at their maximums. Sounds are synthesized with Web Audio. No audio files are used. The second jump resets only on landing. Grapple anchors sit on wall tops and crate tops within 14 units and inside the aim cone, ranked by reticle angle before distance, need a clear line of sight, and detach when the rope is blocked. Melee deals 18 damage once per swing within 2.8 units in 3D, including height, and only with a clear line of sight. Reload takes 1400 ms with 8 poses; ammo transfers only at the loading pose, at 675 ms. Shooting cancels a reload: cancelling before the transfer gives no ammo, cancelling after it keeps the transferred ammo, and the gun recovers to idle.

Settings

Shake scales damage camera shake when reduced motion is off. Flash scales the damage vignette and muzzle flash brightness; zero flash removes those bright layers without hiding hit markers, health changes, sounds, or impact marks. Reduced motion stops damage camera shake, camera roll, and camera zoom, weapon bob, recoil, and sway, air streaks, and HUD motion, while hit markers, health changes, sounds, and impact marks stay as hit and danger cues. There is no hit-stop effect and no camera zoom in this build, so the policy covers them by absence. Volume and mute apply to all synthesized sounds. Audio starts only after Start is clicked. Reduced effect density halves impact particles and hides comic words.
